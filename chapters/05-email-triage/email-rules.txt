EMAIL TRIAGE RULES

SKIP (do not read or process):
- Any email from [your bank]
- Any email from [your doctor/healthcare provider]
- Any email from [HR department]

URGENT (action within 4 hours):
- Emails from: [name 1], [name 2], [name 3] (key clients/boss)
- Subject contains: "deadline", "today", "ASAP", "emergency", "overdue"
- Reply threads older than 3 days where I started the conversation

NEEDS MY REPLY (action within 24 hours):
- Direct questions to me from known contacts
- Meeting requests or scheduling changes
- Client questions about active projects
- Emails with attachments that reference "review" or "approval"

FYI (read when convenient):
- Team status updates
- Newsletters: [list the ones you actually read]
- Tool notifications: Slack digests, calendar reminders
- Industry news from [specific sources]

ARCHIVE (no action needed):
- Marketing emails and promotions
- Social media notifications (LinkedIn, Twitter, etc.)
- Auto-generated order and payment confirmations
- Newsletters: [list the ones you do NOT read]

DRAFT REPLY GUIDELINES:
- Match the sender's tone (casual email = casual reply)
- Keep replies under 100 words unless the topic requires more
- For scheduling: confirm the time, date, and location explicitly
- For questions: answer directly, then offer to discuss further
- For FYI acknowledgments: "Got it, thanks" is fine
