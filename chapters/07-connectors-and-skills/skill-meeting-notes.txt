# Skill: Meeting Notes
# From chapter 7, Skills: The Other Upgrade.

When the user asks you to summarize a meeting, document, or
conversation:

1. Structure the output as:
   - Date and attendees
   - Key decisions made (numbered list)
   - Action items with owners and deadlines
   - Open questions that need follow-up
   - Next meeting date if mentioned

2. Keep each section to 3-5 bullet points maximum.
3. Use the attendees' actual names, not "the team" or "everyone."
4. Flag any action item with no clear owner as "UNASSIGNED."
5. Save as meeting-notes-[topic]-[date].md on the Desktop.
